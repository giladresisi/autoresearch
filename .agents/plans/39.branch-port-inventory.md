### Merge decisions made during the port
- **Executor time rules**: `live`'s `NO_ENTRY_AT_OR_AFTER` / `HARD_CLOSE_AT` / `_enforce_time_rules`
  dropped for plan 38's `ENTRY_CUTOFF_ET` / `WINDOW_END_ET` / `_entry_block` — a superset (it also
  covers an external position change and the after-a-winner rule). `live`'s guard in `_enter` (the
  one site plan 38 does not guard) was re-pointed at `_entry_block`; its other two guards were
  redundant with plan 38's own and were dropped.
- **Actions A and B are UNWIRED here.** The port speaks market entries and market closes only, so
  neither action has a live representation. `Executor._initial_action_is_wired()` refuses both
  unless the order book is the bare simulation; enabling one on a live port is a recorded no-op,
  not a simulated stop move the broker never sees. The 5 mirror-dependent tests were dropped with
  that reason. Default stays `record`.
- **Plan 38 test expectations updated** for two consequences of plan 35: one extra decision record
  (`initial_target_selected`) per fill, and a last-record assertion narrowed to the last
  ORDER-lifecycle record. Their `_build_trader` stubs gained the `date=` argument.
