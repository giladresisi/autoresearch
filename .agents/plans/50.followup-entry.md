# 50. Follow-up entry after the MES-sweep stop (§11.8 CANDIDATE)

Complexity: 🔴 Complex (new entry lifecycle state inside the Executor; two re-anchored
entry triggers; two veto rules suspended). Worktree `../analyze-oct-2`, branch
`analyze-oct-2`, base 3a64173. Operator decisions 2026-10-03: both triggers; only after
the MES-sweep stop; a 20-minute window; a budget of 2 attempts.

## Why

`l2-mechanisms.md` §11.7 (the MES day-extreme sweep stop, ON by default) exits a position
as the retrace starts, at the completed MNQ bar's close less 5. The stop is MEANT to be hit;
in 84% of the studied cases MNQ then continued 15 pts past its pre-retrace peak, and a
profitable exit today ends the plan (`positive_close`), so that continuation is not traded.
The follow-up entry is what makes the sweep stop coherent.

Evidence (`<global>/studies/mes_day_extreme_sweep/followup*.py`, 1s history, 55 stopped
events before 10:30, 46 continued): the retrace low comes a median 2.1 min after the exit
(p75 11 min) and sits a median 41 pts below the exit price; the ideal entry ran 180 pts
(median). Two triggers shaped like §7 and §6, anchored at the retrace low instead of the
day extreme: a reject-close caught 35-46% of continuations at 5-9 R; a post-low 1m FVG
caught 72-87% at 2-3 R. On 2026-10-02 the reject-close fires at 09:58:00 @ 31160.50 — the
operator's own entry. A 20-min window halved the cost on the 9 non-continuing days.

## Scope

Build it as a CANDIDATE (`docs/entry-mechanism-change-protocol.md` step 0): a §11.8
section, a flag, no change to §§2-8 text. The regular mechanisms are untouched; the
follow-up has its own two triggers, its own window and its own budget.

NOT in scope: chaining (a follow-up position's own sweep stop opens no second follow-up);
a follow-up after any other exit kind; tests of the rule's edge (that is the replay A/B,
run later on the operator's request).

## The rule, pinned

Terms: `exit` = the instant the MES-sweep stop was touched (`stop_out_initial`, the
position's stop having last been moved with reason `mes_sweep`), on the FIRST (non
follow-up) position of the plan. Direction = the plan's.

1. **Opening.** At the exit, IF all of: the flag is on; the position was not itself a
   follow-up; the plan has a bound T2 and `remaining = T2 - exit price` (sign-normalised)
   is >= `FOLLOWUP_MIN_T2_PTS` (30) AND >= `FOLLOWUP_MIN_T2_FRACTION` (0.25) x the first
   fill's own distance to T2; no `would_have_falsified` has been recorded for the plan.
   Then the follow-up OPENS: record `followup_opened {exit_px, exit_kind, window_end,
   t2, t2_remaining, first_entry}`. If a condition fails: the plan dies `positive_close`
   exactly as today (after a profitable exit) and a record `followup_skipped {reason}` says why.
2. **Window.** `window_end = min(exit + FOLLOWUP_WINDOW_MIN (20 min), 11:00 ET)`. Inside it
   the follow-up's triggers may fire; `ENTRY_CUTOFF_ET` (10:30) does NOT apply to them
   (same exemption shape as O3's own window). The settle window and `external` apply as
   for any fire.
3. **Budget.** `FOLLOWUP_MAX_ATTEMPTS` = 2, counted on the follow-up itself; a follow-up
   stop-out spends one (and ALSO goes through `_on_stop_out` so §2's cooldown, the
   takeover scan and the shared tally behave as for any stop-out — the plan's
   `attempts_used` is NOT incremented for follow-up attempts). A profitable follow-up exit
   latches the positive close and ends the plan as today.
4. **Deferred death.** While the follow-up is open (window not ended, budget not spent, no
   follow-up position open... or one open) `_positive_close_death` returns None and
   `_entry_block` does not return `after_positive_trade`. When the follow-up CLOSES
   without an open position — window end, budget spent, structure veto — the deferred
   `positive_close` death happens then (`plan_dead reason=positive_close`, detail carries
   `followup: {reason, attempts}`). A follow-up position open at window end is managed to
   its own exit; the plan dies after it.
5. **Structure veto.** The follow-up closes (reason `structure_broken`) the first tick MNQ
   trades beyond the post-09:30 counter-thesis extreme (`post_open_counter_extreme`, the
   leg's origin), and (reason `falsified`) when `would_have_falsified` is recorded during
   the follow-up.
6. **Trigger R — `followup_reject_close`.** A FRESH `extreme_reject.ExtremeReject(direction,
   plan_arm_ts=exit, track="followup", extreme=None)` driven on completed 1m bars from the
   exit's minute on (its first bar sets the extreme; §7's quiet-3 arming and new-extreme
   restart apply unchanged; fire = a new-extreme bar closing with the thesis; stop = the
   wick capped 15). The fire dict's `mechanism` is `followup_reject_close`.
7. **Trigger F — `followup_fvg_1m`.** On each completed 1m bar: the thesis-direction 1m
   FVGs (three-bar, §2 convention: identity = middle bar, existence = third-bar
   completion) whose FIRST bar opens at or after the exit's minute. The first such gap
   not yet attempted fires on its third bar's completion: entry = that bar's close
   (market), stop = the lowest low (highest high) of the three bars - 0.25, capped at 25
   pts (§6's cap). Gaps smaller than 1 tick are ignored.
8. **Arbitration and entry.** Both triggers are evaluated on the bar-close path only,
   through the existing `fires -> pick -> market entry` flow, with the SAME preconditions
   as `_drive_market_mechanisms` EXCEPT: `entry_block == "after_positive_trade"` and
   `"entry_cutoff"` are not blocks for them, and the §2 extension veto is NOT applied
   (its anchor is the 09:30 counter-extreme; the follow-up's leg starts at the retrace
   low). `_in_cooldown` applies. The regular mechanisms (§6, §7, tmso, fvg_1h, micro-SMT
   entry) do NOT fire while the follow-up is open: the follow-up is the only entry path then.
9. **Target.** The follow-up fill takes the first fill's T2 through the existing
   `_reused_first_pick` path (O6). Verify, do not reimplement. Break-even at 50%, the
   initial-target stage, O4 and the MES-sweep stop manage the follow-up position as any
   other (the sweep stage is rebuilt per position; its exit on a follow-up position opens
   NO second follow-up).
10. **Flag.** `ACT_FOLLOWUP_ENTRY`: unset/empty = ON; `0`/`false`/`no`/`off` = off (then
    today's behaviour exactly: the exit ends the plan). Read once per Executor;
    `followup.FOLLOWUP_ENABLED` (None/True/False) overrides for a harness.
11. **Records** (decision stream, bar time): `followup_opened`, `followup_skipped`,
    `followup_closed {reason: window_end | attempts_exhausted | structure_broken |
    falsified | positive_close}`, and every fire / veto / fill with
    `mechanism=followup_reject_close | followup_fvg_1m` and `followup: true`.
