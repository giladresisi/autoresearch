# 35 — Initial-target stage for the trader graft's position management

**Complexity:** ⚠️ Medium
**Status:** selection rule v2 (this document) supersedes the v1 rule that was implemented and
measured on 2026-09-20; v2 is IMPLEMENTED (2026-09-20, unstaged on `live`): selector, level
universe, fixtures, 30-day rerun, default frozen to A + session_mid_first (§5). The post-flip
ACTION stays `record` (observation only); changing management after the flip is a later,
separate decision.
**Origin:** session-analysis 2026-09-18 O1 (operator-proposed); v2 from the 30-day lookahead study
(`agent/study/initial-target-lookahead.md`) and the operator's per-day review of it.

## 1. What exists today

- The Executor (`agent/trader/executor.py`) holds two numbers for an open position: the mechanism's
  fixed stop and ONE T2 target picked at the fill (`_set_target_on_fill` → `target.select_target`,
  the D1 menu row). Exits: broker stop, market close on a tick touch of the target (`OrderSim.on_bar`
  → `take_profit`; no close-beyond is required), the 13:00 hard close, or a manual close mirrored
  silently as `user_close`. No breakeven, trail, partial, cautious or initial target existed before
  this plan.
- Cycle-5 (`plans/33`, commit 555de09) measured management rules over 66 sessions: "no management,
  stop only" ranked first; breakeven-as-clamp-to-fill was refuted at every stop width. Any action
  taken at the initial target must beat that control on the same rig.
- The legacy initial target (what the operator wants reproduced in spirit):
  - Selection — `hypothesis.compute_cautious_prices` (hypothesis.py:99): candidates on the trade
    side of the fill within 150 pts; secondary = the farthest, initial = the candidate closest to
    the secondary within 110 pts and ≥ 40 pts from the fill, offset 2 pts toward the fill; else the
    synthetic 85 % fallback.
  - Reached rule — `trend.py:450-454, 525-531`: `_surpassed` (tick: bar LOW ≤ level for a short /
    HIGH ≥ for a long) AND `_close_beyond` (1m CLOSE beyond the level). A touch alone flips nothing.
    One-way flip.

### 1.1 What the v1 implementation and the 30-day study found (2026-09-20)

v1 ported the legacy rule verbatim onto the facts-layer level universe. The lookahead study
(30 days, correct 09:30 direction, optimal entry in 09:30:30–10:30, production selectors, no stop):
- The v1 selector admitted WRONG-SIDE levels (a `_low` for a long, a `_high` for a short): 4 of the 6
  days reviewed by hand picked one (`prev4_day_low` 09-01 long, `prev2_week_low` 08-27 long,
  `prev6_day_high` 08-12 short, `prev3_day_high` 09-08 short). The T2 menu never does that.
- The fixed 110-pt cap excluded every level the operator judged relevant: NY-morning extremes at
  218 / 206 / 256 / 153 pts on 09-01 / 08-27 / 08-12 / 09-08. T2 is a median 161 pts away (often
  250–450 when TDO is the D1 pick, 19/30 days); legacy sized 110 against a ≤150-pt secondary.
- The 40-pt floor was tested on the raw level, before the 2-pt offset → 38.25 / 39.25-pt initials.
- Touch-only (touched, no 1m close beyond) occurred 0/30: the close-beyond clause only delays the
  flip.
- With v1 picks, exiting at the flip kept a median 54 % of MFE on failed-primary days and preceded T2
  on 20/20 winners. That verdict is about v1's levels; it is re-measured after v2.

## 2. Design

### 2.1 Where it lives
`agent/trader/initial_target.py` (pure: selection + reached-state machine), driven by the Executor
per bar like `target.py`. No legacy imports (`agent/trader/__init__.py` boundary): the selection is
implemented on the facts layer, never by calling `hypothesis.compute_cautious_prices`.

### 2.2 Inputs
- Anchor *P* = the fill price at `_set_target_on_fill` time.
- Secondary *S* = **the T2 target already picked** (`_target_price`), exact (no 5-pt offset).
  D = |S − P|.
- Candidate universe = `target.level_universe(bars, now)` (the bundle's named levels + day/week
  hi/lo/mid + the in-progress 6h block's extremes) **plus two families added for v2**:
  - `rth(cur)_high` / `rth(cur)_low`: the running post-09:30 extreme, from the 09:30 bar through the
    last COMPLETED bar before the fill (the entry bar itself is excluded).
  - `ny_morning(cur)_mid`: the midpoint of the running NY-morning block's high and low.
- **Eligibility (v2 — the same rules the T2 menu applies, `derive_facts._dol_menu`):**
  1. strictly between *P* and *S*;
  2. **correct side**: for a LONG only `*_high` levels, mids and opens; for a SHORT only `*_low`
     levels, mids and opens. Mids/opens = `day_mid`, `week_mid`, `ny_morning(cur)_mid`, `TDO`,
     `TWO`. A `_low` is never an initial for a long, a `_high` never for a short;
  3. not swept and not depleted (the validator flags `level_universe` already carries);
  4. not nested: exclude names in the bundle's `suppressed_p1_levels["MNQ"]` (the same set the DOL
     menu excludes);
  5. **distance band as a fraction of D**, replacing the fixed 110-pt cap:
     `0.20·D ≤ along ≤ 0.80·D`, where `along` is the signed distance from *P* along the trade;
  6. floor applied to the OFFSET price: `along − 2 ≥ 40` (fixes the sub-40 initials).
- Shrink per failed attempt is dropped in v2 (the band already scales with D; the legacy shrink
  addressed a fixed cap). `attempts_used` is still recorded.

### 2.3 Selection (v2)
Tiered by the kind of structure, then by distance within the tier. Two knobs are left as
PARAMETERS settled by the 30-day rerun (§5), not by the six hand-reviewed days:
```
EXTREME_MIN      : a session extreme (tier 1) qualifies only if it is far enough from P —
                   variant F: along ≥ 0.65·D   |   variant A: along ≥ 150 pts   (see below)
MID_PREFERENCE   : within tier 2 — "nearest" | "farthest" | "session_mid_first"

tier 1  session extremes in the trade direction that pass EXTREME_MIN and the band:
        rth(cur)_{high|low}, ny_morning(cur)_…, london(cur)_…, asia(cur)_…
        → pick the MOST RECENT 6h session's extreme (rth > ny_morning > london > asia)   [decided]
tier 2  mids/opens: ny_morning(cur)_mid, day_mid, week_mid, TDO, TWO → per MID_PREFERENCE
tier 3  prevN day/week levels (correct side, not nested) → pick the farthest from P
fallback: P ± 0.85·D  (label synthetic_85pct), if 0.85·D − 2 ≥ 40; else None (no stage)
initial = level ∓ 2 pts toward P
```
Why EXTREME_MIN is a parameter: on the reviewed days the operator wanted the session extreme at
49 % (09-01), 73 % (09-08) and 76 % (08-27) of D and NOT at 54 % (08-25) and 55 % (09-15). No
fraction separates 49 from 54, so variant F (0.65) loses 09-01 to tier 2; the absolute distances
218 / 153 / 181 vs 137 / 121 pts DO separate (variant A, ≥150 pts) but on a 16-pt margin fitted to
five days. Why MID_PREFERENCE is a parameter: 08-12 wants the NY-morning mid (42 %) over day_mid
(63 %); 08-25 wants day_mid (78 %) over the NY-morning mid (27 %) and week_mid (68 %).
Recorded as `initial_target_selected {price, level, level_price, tier, secondary, anchor, band,
n_candidates, variant}`. `None` means no initial stage.

### 2.4 Reached rule (state machine, per COMPLETED 1m bar — not per tick)
`reached` flips once when a completed 1m bar satisfies BOTH: `low ≤ initial` (short) / `high ≥`
(long) **and** `close < initial` / `close >`. Same-bar precedence: if that bar also touches the stop,
the stop wins. Recorded as `initial_target_reached {bar, price, close, mechanism}`. Kept as-is even
though the study saw no touch-only day in 30: it is the operator's rule and costs nothing.

### 2.5 Post-flip ACTION
`INITIAL_TARGET_ACTION = "record"` (default and the only shipped behaviour). `be_structure` (A: stop
to the initial level, once, via the mirror's `move-stop-exit` → `live_orders.update_stop_loss`) and
`opp_close` (B: market close on the first opposite 1m close after the flip, reason
`initial-target-opp-close`) exist behind the constant for the study rig only. A profit-side stop
touch under A books `stop_out_initial` and spends no attempt. Changing management after the flip is
NOT part of this plan; the operator will specify it later.

### 2.6 Related follow-ups (not in this plan)
- Resting take-profit limit at T2 at the broker (PMT `update_tp` right after the fill) instead of the
  per-second touch → market close. Catches intrabar spikes (08-25 10:30 low 29157.75 vs T2 29164) and
  aligns the broker fill with the simulator's touch price. Needs a PMT smoke test like `update_sl`.
- T2 is TDO on 19/30 days; when T2 is far the 85 % fallback lands 280–320 pts out and is inert. Not
  an initial-target defect; a target-selection topic (`l2-target-selection.md`).

## 4. Tests (enumerated)
- Selection v2: side filter (a `_low` is never picked for a long; a `_high` never for a short);
  swept/depleted excluded; nested excluded via `suppressed`; band 20–80 % of D (a level at 85 % is
  out, at 79 % in); floor after offset (a 41-pt level → None/fallback, a 42-pt level → 40.0 initial);
  tier order (session extreme beats a nearer mid; mid beats a farther prevN); tier-1 farthest vs
  tier-2 nearest; fallback when no tier qualifies; None when 0.85·D − 2 < 40; long-side mirror.
- The six reviewed days as fixtures on their recorded level lists: 09-01 → ny_morning(cur)_high
  29258; 08-27 → rth(cur)_high 29605; 08-12 → ny_morning(cur)_mid 29873.25; 09-08 →
  ny_morning(cur)_low 29534 (pending decision (b)); 09-15 and 08-25 per decision (a).
- `level_universe`: `rth(cur)` excludes the entry bar; `ny_morning(cur)_mid` = (hi+lo)/2 of the
  running block; nothing emitted before 09:30.
- Reached rule, Executor ordering, actions A/B/C, mirror emissions, no emission after `user_close`:
  unchanged from v1 (28 tests) and must stay green.
