# Cycle 4 Phase 2 — Candidate universe and labelling

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use `superpowers:subagent-driven-development`
> or `superpowers:executing-plans` to implement this task-by-task. Steps use `- [ ]` syntax.

**Goal:** For each of the 87 sessions, name the candidate the 09:30 move was drawn to,
record every candidate it passed through or fell short of, and report honestly how much of
that is structure rather than census.

**Architecture:** Offline analysis, no Executor code. The candidate universe comes from
`derive_facts.compute_facts` through the existing `agent/bench/facts.bundle_for_boundary`
slicing rule (never a second copy of it), evaluated at each move's **move-start**. Labelling
is a pure function of that universe plus the skeleton's move window plus the 1m bars.

**Tech Stack:** Python, pandas, pytest. `agent/study/` (new modules), `scripts/` (CLIs),
`.agents/label-corpus/` (outputs).

**Spec:** `.agents/plans/26.cycle4-target-selection.md` — §§2, 3, 4 are load-bearing here.
Read §2's SETTLED table, §3.4 (attractiveness hypothesis), §4.2 (the three disciplines) and
§4.4 (the censored four) before starting.

**Complexity:** 🔴 Complex — 6 tasks. The risk is not the code; it is defining the draw rule

## Findings from the feasibility probes (2026-09-03) — these shaped the plan

**1. The study must use the 1m primary slice, not the 1s one.** `MNQ_1s.parquet` /
`MES_1s.parquet` under `<main>/2026-09/` begin at **2026-05-01**, so the 17-day lookback
`bundle_for_boundary` takes is empty at the start of the study range. At the 2026-05-01 09:35
boundary the 1s path yields **4 levels**; the 1m path yields **26**, because `MNQ_1m.parquet`
carries history back to 2024-01-01. Using 1s would silently truncate the candidate universe
across the first three weeks of the corpus.

Where both are available the two agree exactly. At 2026-08-13 09:35 and 2026-08-11 09:35 the
level sets, level prices and sweep timestamps are **identical**; `avg_range_1h` differs by 0.1
(52.5 vs 52.4). This is expected rather than lucky — sweeps are wick tests (`low <= level`),
and a 1m bar's high/low is the max/min of its constituent 1s bars, so the crossing minute is
preserved and only sub-minute ordering is lost. Task 1 locks this as a test.

1m is also ~8× faster (4.2s vs 38.7s per boundary), which puts a full 87-session build at
roughly 7 minutes.

**2. The entry instant must not be a single number.** At the 2026-08-13 09:40 boundary —
move-start + 10 minutes — MNQ is already at 30038.50, against a move-start of 29862.75. The
confirmation tax at that proxy is **175.75 points, 43% of the whole 404-point move**, leaving
only 34.75 points to the DOL: a distance that `DOL_FLOOR_PTS = 60` would veto outright. A
10-minute proxy is not a proxy for what the Executor does; it is a different strategy.

So the plan does **not** pick one entry proxy. Eligibility is anchored at **move-start**, which
is structural and proxy-free, and distance is reported at a *curve* of proxies (+2, +5, +10
min). The confirmation-tax curve becomes a phase-2 output rather than a buried assumption.

**3. The 08-13 universe confirms the draw rule before it is written.** At the 09:30 boundary
the unswept MNQ pools above are, in order: prev7_day_high 29956.50, prev3_day_high 29985.00,
prev1_day_high 30001.50, then **prev1_week_high = prev6_day_high = 30073.25**. The move topped
at 30267.00, so the last candidate it reached is prev1_week_high — 193.75 points of overshoot.
That is exactly the answer spec §2.4 established by hand. Note also that two names share one
price: price-level dedup with tier promotion is required, not optional.

## Definitions fixed now, before any outcome is examined

These are additions to spec 26 §2 and must be copied into it (Task 6, step 1) so the spec
stays the single record.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Evaluation instant** | **move-start**. The universe is built at `bundle_for_boundary(..., move_start_ts)`, which slices strictly `<` the boundary. Structural (§2.1), proxy-free, and the same anchor the phase-3 backward walk uses. |
| **Entry-proxy curve** | MNQ 1m close at move-start + 2, + 5 and + 10 minutes. Used **only** to report distance and confirmation tax. Never for eligibility, never for labelling. |
| **Eligible candidate** | A candidate whose price is *ahead* of the move-start price in the move's direction, and which is not already swept/depleted at the evaluation instant. |
| **Reached** | The candidate's own ticker wick-crossed its price at some point inside the move window `[move_start_ts, extreme_ts]`, engine convention (`low <= level` / `high >= level`), via `derive_facts.first_cross`. |
| **The draw (label)** | (1) Among eligible candidates **reached** inside the window, on **either** ticker: the one reached **last**. (2) If none was reached: the **nearest** eligible candidate whose `closest_approach` falls inside the tolerance band. (3) Otherwise the session is **unexplained**. |
| **Why "last reached" and not a coincidence window** | "Completion coinciding with the turn" needs no free parameter: the move ran through everything nearer and stopped at the last thing it completed. A tolerance-in-minutes would be a second 0.382. The **lag** (`extreme_ts − reached_ts`) is recorded as a *result*, and its distribution is what would later justify a window — with a date, in the spec. |
| **Why near-misses rank by distance, not time** | An unreached candidate's `closest_approach` timestamp is, by construction, the move extreme itself. Ranking near-misses by time would hand them every session. They compete only when nothing was reached, and then on distance. |
| **Tolerance band** | `0.15 × avg_range_1h[ticker]` from the same bundle, per §2. Sensitivity reported at 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.25. Applied only to the near-miss arbitration above. Never cross-instrument (§2.3), never to test proximity to move-end (§2.4). |
| **Ambiguous** | Two or more candidates reached in the same 1m bar at distinct prices, or two distinct classes sharing one price after dedup. Reported, never forced. |
| **Counterpart status** | For the labelled candidate on ticker A, the status on ticker B of the *same-named* candidate: `reached`, `near_miss_in_band`, `near_miss_out_of_band`, `absent`. `near_miss_in_band` is the §3.1 phenomenon — the SMT pointed at the target — and it is a first-class field. |
| **Holdout** | ISO week number mod 4 == 0 → held back. Fixed now, without looking at any outcome, and written to `.agents/label-corpus/holdout.json`. |

```python
"""1m-backed facts source for the cycle-4 study.

Why 1m and not the 1s parquets production uses: `<main>/2026-09/{MNQ,MES}_1s.parquet`
begin at 2026-05-01, so `bundle_for_boundary`'s 17-day lookback is empty at the start
of the study range -- 4 levels at the 2026-05-01 boundary against 26 from the 1m
frames, which carry history to 2024. Where both have history they agree exactly
(test_1m_and_1s_agree_where_both_have_history): sweeps are wick tests and a 1m bar's
high/low is the max/min of its constituent 1s bars, so the crossing minute survives.

The slicing rule itself is NOT reimplemented here. `bundle_for_boundary` is the one
boundary->slice rule shared by the online assembler and the offline bench, and a second
copy of it is a failure this project has already paid for twice.
"""
from __future__ import annotations

import os

import pandas as pd

import paths
from agent.bench.facts import bundle_for_boundary
from derive_facts import TZ, load

CONTRACT_SUBDIR = "2026-09"


class StudyFacts:
    def __init__(self, main_dir: "str | None" = None, tickers=("MNQ", "MES")) -> None:
        self.main_dir = main_dir or os.path.join(paths.general_main_dir(), CONTRACT_SUBDIR)
        self.tickers = tuple(tickers)
        self._norm, self._raw, self._cache = {}, {}, {}
        for tk in self.tickers:
            self._norm[tk] = load(self.source_path(tk))
            self._raw[tk] = self._load_raw(self.source_path(tk))

    def source_path(self, ticker: str) -> str:
        return os.path.join(self.main_dir, f"{ticker}_1m.parquet")

    @staticmethod
    def _load_raw(path: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
        df = pd.read_parquet(path)
        df.index = pd.to_datetime(df.index)
        df.index = (df.index.tz_localize(TZ) if df.index.tz is None
                    else df.index.tz_convert(TZ))
        return df.sort_index()

    def bars_1m(self, ticker: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
        return self._norm[ticker]

    def bundle_at(self, boundary: pd.Timestamp):
        """The FactsBundle as of strictly before `boundary`. Cached: a session needs
        the same boundary several times and each build costs ~4s."""
        key = boundary.value
        if key not in self._cache:
            bundle, _ = bundle_for_boundary(self._raw, self._norm, boundary,
                                            tickers=self.tickers)
            self._cache[key] = bundle
        return self._cache[key]
```

## Task 3: The draw rule

**Files:**
- Create: `agent/study/labelling.py`
- Test: `agent/study/test_labelling.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Candidate` (Task 2), a `FactsBundle`, per-ticker 1m frames (Task 1), and one
  skeleton segment (`agent.study.skeleton.Segment`, already committed).
- Produces:

```python
OUTCOME_REACHED_PASSED = "reached_passed"
OUTCOME_DRAW           = "draw"
OUTCOME_NEAR_MISS_IN   = "near_miss_in_band"
OUTCOME_NEAR_MISS_OUT  = "near_miss_out_of_band"
OUTCOME_INELIGIBLE     = "ineligible"
STATUS_LABELLED    = "labelled"
STATUS_UNEXPLAINED = "unexplained"

def label_segment(segment, bundle, bars_by_ticker, candidates, *,
                  band_mult=0.15) -> tuple:      # -> (Label, list[CandidateRow])
```

`Label` fields: `status`, `ticker`, `names`, `price`, `cls`, `tier`, `reached_ts`,
`lag_min`, `overshoot`, `via` (`"reached_last"` | `"near_miss"`), `ambiguous`,
`counterpart_status`, `dist_from_start`, `dist_at_plus2`, `dist_at_plus5`,
`dist_at_plus10`, `band`.

`CandidateRow` fields: `ticker`, `names`, `price`, `cls`, `tier`, `outcome`, `reached_ts`,
`closest_approach`, `dist_from_start`.

**The rule, restated so an implementer cannot drift from it:**

1. **Eligible** = ahead of `segment.start_price` in `segment.direction`, and `swept_before`
   is False. Everything else gets `OUTCOME_INELIGIBLE` and takes no further part.
2. On each candidate's **own ticker's** 1m bars restricted to `[start_ts, extreme_ts]` — the
   same wall interval for both tickers, because it is one event in time and two price spaces
   — compute `first_cross(frame, price, side)`, `side="above"` for an up move and `"below"`
   for a down move.
3. If any eligible candidate was crossed: **the draw is the one with the latest cross
   timestamp.** Every other crossed candidate is `OUTCOME_REACHED_PASSED`. `via="reached_last"`.
4. If none was crossed: compute `closest_approach` for each eligible candidate; the draw is
   the **nearest** one inside `band = band_mult * bundle.avg_range_1h[candidate.ticker]`,
   `via="near_miss"`. Others inside the band get `OUTCOME_NEAR_MISS_IN`; outside,
   `OUTCOME_NEAR_MISS_OUT`.
5. If nothing qualifies: `status = STATUS_UNEXPLAINED`, no label price, and the candidate
   rows are still emitted in full.
6. `ambiguous` is True when two or more distinct candidate prices were crossed in the same
   1m bar as the winner.
7. `counterpart_status` looks up the label's names on the *other* ticker and reports that
   candidate's own outcome, or `"absent"` when the other ticker has no such name.

`overshoot` (signed distance label → `extreme_price`) and `lag_min` (`extreme_ts −
reached_ts`) are **result fields**: both are computed from the move's end, which is lookahead.
The module docstring must say so, and no phase-3 predictor may read them.

## Task 5: The null models

**Files:**
- Create: `agent/study/nulls.py`
- Test: `agent/study/test_nulls.py`

Two nulls, per spec §4.2 and §3.4, reported alongside every headline number.

**Null A — is the label closer to the turn than chance?** The rule names *something* whenever
anything was reached, so its hit rate means nothing on its own. What is testable is the
labelled candidate's `|overshoot|` against the `|distance to move-end|` of a uniformly-drawn
eligible candidate from the same session. If the draw is no closer to the extreme than a
random eligible pool, the label is census rather than structure.

**Null B — per-class pull vs per-class census.** A class that is 40% of eligible candidates
and 40% of draws has no pull. Report `draw_share − eligible_share` per class with a bootstrap
interval.

Determinism: a module-level `SEED = 20260903` and `numpy.random.default_rng(SEED)`. An
unseeded draw makes the report unreproducible, which for this project is the same as untrue.

## Execution notes — deviations found while building (2026-09-03)

Two of the plan's own definitions were wrong and the real-session gates caught both. Neither
was tuned into agreement; both are corrections to the implementation of definitions the spec
already fixed.

### D1 — one label per INSTRUMENT, not one per session (spec-conformance error)

The plan's Task 3 rule said "among eligible candidates reached inside the window, on **either**
ticker, the one reached last". That collapses two DOLs into one and violates spec §2.4's
role-swap invariance: whichever graph does the sweeping, MNQ's DOL is still an MNQ pool.

2026-08-13 exposed it immediately. MNQ reaches its own `prev1_week_high` (30073.25) at 09:46;
MES reaches its own `prev1_week_high` (7820.25) at 09:58. A global "latest wins" therefore
labelled MNQ's session with an MES price — and in doing so destroyed the actual finding, which
is that **both instruments independently name the same level**. That correspondence is spec
§2.4's claim, now measured rather than asserted.

`label_segment` returns `{ticker: Label}`. The cross-instrument reading (spec §3.1) is
*recorded* rather than substituted: `is_halt_driver` marks the instrument whose draw completed
last, `counterpart_status` says what the other instrument did at the same named level.

### D2 — the move window must run to the END of the extreme bar (bug)

The skeleton is built on 5m bars, so a segment's `extreme_ts` is the OPEN of the bar the
extreme printed in. The first implementation sliced `[start_ts, extreme_ts]`, truncating the
last bar to its first minute.

On 2026-08-11 the extreme bar opens 09:45 and the day-low sweep prints at 09:46. The truncated
window therefore had a low of 29678.75 against the segment's actual 29636.00, missed the sweep
entirely, and labelled `london(cur)_low` at 29717.50 — 81 points short of the level the session
is known for. With the fix the label is `asia(cur)_low` = 29666.00, reached with 30.0 points of
overshoot, which is exactly the hand-established reading.

### O1 — OPEN: the tolerance band does not reach the one hand-established near-miss

On 2026-08-11 MES fell **5.0 points** short of its own `asia(cur)_low` while MNQ swept through
its equivalent — the bullish SMT the session turned on. `avg_range_1h[MES]` at that boundary is
9.8, so the §2 band of `0.15 x avg_range_1h` is **1.47 points** and the miss reads as
`near_miss_out_of_band`. It is not an MES artefact: 5 MES points is ~19 MNQ points against an
MNQ band of 10.17, so the same structural miss is out of band in both price spaces. Catching it
needs roughly `0.5 x avg_range_1h`.

This changes nothing that has been built. The band is a SETTLED value (spec §2) and was not
touched; the corpus stores `closest_approach` for every candidate, so the band is a **reporting
threshold** and the sensitivity sweep (report item 9, extended to 0.50) answers it without a
re-run. Two things follow for the human gate:

- The band's labelling job (arbitrating a near-miss when nothing was reached) and its
  descriptive job (classifying the counterpart instrument) may want **different** thresholds.
  A near-miss the move never reached is a weaker claim than an SMT between two instruments.
- Any change to either is a dated spec revision under §2, not an edit here.

### O2 — three of the four censored sessions landed in the holdout

The split was fixed on ISO week mod 4 before anything was labelled, and 2026-05-12, 06-09 and
08-04 fall in held-back weeks. Noted, not adjusted: changing a holdout after seeing what landed
in it is the precise failure the split exists to prevent.

## Gate Verdict — findings that must be settled before phase 3

**G1. The per-class pull is confounded by proximity, and the confound is total.** Report item
5 shows `session_extreme` and `open_price` drawing far beyond their census while `day_extreme`
and `week_extreme` are avoided. But the mean normalised distance rank per class (0 = nearest
eligible pool ahead, 1 = furthest) is:

| class | MNQ | MES |
|---|---|---|
| open_price | 0.13 | 0.14 |
| session_extreme | 0.21 | 0.19 |
| mid | 0.16 | 0.21 |
| day_extreme | 0.52 | 0.50 |
| week_extreme | 0.81 | 0.83 |

That is the **same ordering as the pull ranking**, exactly. The classes that "pull" are the
classes that sit nearest, and a rule that stops at the last pool it reaches will name near
pools disproportionately whatever their tier. Spec §3.4's hypothesis is NOT supported by this
report and is not refuted by it either — it has not yet been tested. The test is per-class
draw share **conditioned on distance rank** (or on distance bucket in `avg_range_1h` units),
and it belongs at the head of phase 3.

**G2. Null A is real but partly mechanical.** Observed median |move-end − draw| is 59.9pt
against a null median of 220.1pt, `beats_null_frac = 1.0`. But "last reached" is by
construction the furthest-along candidate the move crossed, so it is necessarily closer to the
extreme than a pool drawn from the whole eligible set — which includes every pool the move
never approached. The honest null draws only from **reached** candidates. Until that is run,
Null A confirms the rule is not random and nothing stronger.

**G3. The near-miss branch is nearly unused, which retires the band's urgency.** 2 of 64 MNQ
labels and 2 of 59 MES labels came through it. The band therefore has almost no influence on
labelling; its influence is entirely on the residual (0.15 → 0.50 rescues 6 MNQ sessions and 5
MES). The open question from O1 stands but is no longer on the critical path: the band's
*descriptive* job — classifying what the counterpart instrument did — is where a wider value
would change conclusions, and `near_miss_in_band` fires exactly **once** at 0.15 across 84
sessions.

**G4. The unexplained residual is real, not an artefact.** For the 16 MNQ unexplained sessions
with eligible candidates, the nearest unswept pool beyond move-end sits a median **0.85 x
avg_range_1h** away, and 4 of them stopped more than 2 x avg_range_1h from anything. Only 4 had
no eligible candidate at all. These moves stopped in genuinely empty space. 74% of all
candidates are ineligible at move-start — mostly already swept — so depletion is doing much of
the work the universe appears to be doing.

**G5. "Coincidence" is looser than the word suggests.** Lag median 12 minutes, p90 44, and
16 of 62 MNQ labels were reached more than 30 minutes before the turn. Combined with the
overshoot median of 0.7 x avg_range_1h, the shape of a typical session is: complete the last
pool, then run on for another ten minutes and most of an hourly range. That is consistent with
spec §2.4 and it means the draw is a *destination the move passes through*, not a place it
stops. Any later rule that assumes proximity in time between completion and turn is
unsupported by this corpus.

**G6. The cross-instrument correspondence is weaker than 2026-08-13 suggested.** Both
instruments named the same level on only 18 of 84 segments, and the MES-fell-short-while-
MNQ-swept pattern registers in-band once. 08-13 remains a clean example; it is not the norm.

Carried into phase 3: G1 (condition class on distance before believing it), G2 (the tighter
null), and the confirmation-tax curve as the empirical basis for a reasonable-distance rule
and for revisiting `DOL_FLOOR_PTS`.
