# 36. Cross-asset SMT in the entry layer: a gate on §7, and a named-level two-bar reject

⚠️ Medium — the code is small; the prerequisites and the measurement are the work.

## Goal

Bring the MNQ/MES divergence below L1 for the first time. Two configurations came out of
the measurement, and **they are not two rules on one mechanism** — they sit on different
anchors:

| | anchor | accept test | SMT definition | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | running post-09:30 extreme | **R1** — the new-extreme bar closes WITH the thesis | MES did not make a new running extreme on that bar | **a gate on §7**, which already is anchor+R1 |
| **B** | a NAMED level (`ny_morning` extreme) | **R2** — the new-extreme bar closes AGAINST, the NEXT bar closes with | MES has not swept its own copy of that level | **a new mechanism** |

## The measurements

Signal-level, 92 sessions, direction from the tape's oracle. Policy: stop = swept extreme
-/+ 3 capped at 15 pts from entry (§7's own cap), held 45 minutes or to the stop.

**Running-extreme anchor, 311 fires:**

```
rule  smt      n   surv   pnl        win%   pnl/fire
R1    False   108   18    +1904.50   17%    +17.63
R1    True     78   21    +3197.75   27%    +41.00    <-- A
R2    False    72   15    +1171.25   21%    +16.27
R2    True     53   11    +1038.75   21%    +19.60
```

**Named-level anchor, 70 fires:**

```
rule  smt      n   surv   pnl        win%   pnl/fire
R1    False    17    4     +823.00   24%    +48.41
R1    True     11    0     -163.75    0%    -14.89
R2    False    20    3     +203.25   15%    +10.16
R2    True     22    8     +716.25   36%    +32.56    <-- B
```

**The pairing inverts between the two anchors** — the running-extreme SMT helps R1 and does
nothing for R2; the named-level SMT helps R2 and destroys R1. There is no single "SMT
filter"; there are two signals and each pairs with one rejection shape.

By level, among named-level SMT fires: `ny_morning` n=13, +510.25 (+39.25/fire); `asia`
n=13, +23.25; `london` n=6, +34.00; `prev_day` n=1. **B is an ny_morning mechanism.**

**Pooling A and B under one SMT filter is REFUTED**: R1+R2 with the named SMT gives
33 fires / +552.50 / +16.74 per fire, against 37 fires / +1026.25 / +27.74 without it. The
pooled form drags R1's eleven 0-for-11 fires along with R2's good ones.

Motivating days: 08-31 fires B at 09:33:00 (+52.25, never stopped) — the entry no current
mechanism can take. 09-02 fires A at 09:56:00 (+164.25, never stopped).

**CORRECTION (2026-09-15), read before executing.** An earlier draft of this line claimed
09-01 "fires A at 09:43:00". It does not. On 09-01 the running-extreme anchor gives:

    R2  09:33:00  -15.00      R1  09:40:00  -15.00      R2  09:43:00  +94.75

so the +94.75 winner is **R2 on the running-extreme anchor** — which is NEITHER A (R1 on
that anchor) nor B (R2 on a NAMED level). A fires at 09:40 and loses. That third

**AND IT COULD NOT FIRE IT ANYWAY.** Under the shipped stretch override, 09-01 runs UP and

competing for the same three attempts, so on this day they can only consume the budget
EARLIER, not later. Fixing 09-01 is a budget problem first and a mechanism problem second,
and the budget is on this plan's own out-of-scope list.

## D0 — MEASURE AT ENGINE LEVEL FIRST. Do not write A or B before this.

Every number above is signal-level: no attempt budget, no single-position rule, no T2
target, no `target_reached` death, and 3.4 fires per session against a 3-attempt budget.

**The precedent is exact and recent.** `fvg_1h_reject` fired 6 times in its signal scan and
**0 times in 29 engine sessions** — other mechanisms took the entry first, or the budget was
gone. A mechanism can be right in isolation and inert in the engine.

D0 is: replay N >= 29 sessions with the oracle direction injected (no model calls —
`run_replay(..., thesis=...)`), three arms — baseline, +A, +A+B — and compare realised
P&L, fires per mechanism, and attempts consumed. The existing driver is
`scratchpad/entries_scan.py`; fold it into `agent/study/` if it is to be kept.

**Stop rule:** if A fires fewer than ~1 time per 3 sessions in the engine, or if adding it
does not improve realised P&L, it does not ship regardless of the signal-level table.

## Prerequisites (both are design decisions, not typing)

**P1 — MES must reach the mechanism layer.** Nothing below L1 looks at MES today: the
Executor is MNQ-only (`self._ticker`, `_gaps_on`, every driver takes `mnq`). `bars` already
carries both, so this is plumbing, but it is a new capability and should be decided once,
for all mechanisms, not smuggled in per-mechanism.

**P2 — where named levels come from.** They exist in the facts (`liquidities`), but
`tmso_reject` and `fvg_1h_reject` both compute their own anchors from bars instead —
deliberately, to avoid re-keying the thesis cache. B needs `ny_morning` for BOTH assets.
Decide: compute from bars (consistent with the two existing mechanisms, no cache impact) or
consume the facts store (one source of truth, but MES levels are not built today — see
`build_menus`'s MNQ-only note). **Recommendation: compute from bars**, matching precedent.

## D1 — A: the SMT gate on §7

`extreme_reject.py` gains an optional cross-asset gate: when the new-extreme bar fires R1,
suppress the fire if MES made a new running extreme on the same bar.

Default OFF behind a flag until D0 says otherwise. `ExtremeReject` is committed, measured,
and has no named-case coverage of its wired behaviour — changing its fire condition
unconditionally is the risk flagged repeatedly in this cycle.

## D2 — B: `ny_morning_reject`

A new market mechanism: MNQ's 1m bar sweeps the `ny_morning` extreme for the first time;
the bar closes against the thesis; the next bar closes with it; MES has not swept its own
`ny_morning` copy. Entry at the confirming bar's close, stop at the swept extreme -/+ 3
capped 15.

Reuses `reject_core` for the colour predicate and the capped stop. Registered like
`tmso_reject`: a market mechanism in `MarketMechanisms`, a self-gating class in
`planner.MECHANISM_CLASSES`.

## Out of scope

- **Generalising the sweep-then-reject vocabulary.** Deferred deliberately: too many
  nuances across §6, §7, `tmso_reject`, `fvg_1h_reject` and B, and the existing ones have
  not yet earned it. `reject_core` stays a two-function helper and is NOT extended into a
  shared machine here.
- **Refactoring §6 or §7 beyond D1's flag.** Their constants trace to recorded A/Bs and
  they have no named-case coverage; a refactor is currently unfalsifiable.
- The 07:00-FVG suppression veto (base rate 3-in-94, two resolving instantly).
- Pooled R1+R2+SMT (measured worse than no filter).
- Any timing or budget gate. Three have now been measured and refuted: the fast-stop-out
  penalty (a fast stop-out predicts a BETTER next entry — 28% vs 18%), attempt caps
  (attempts 2-3 are worth +661.50 over 29 sessions), and stretch-conditioned early
  suppression.
