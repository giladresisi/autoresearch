# Cycle 4 Plan 30 — re-deriving the target rules at a production-computable instant

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: `superpowers:executing-plans`. Steps use `- [ ]`.

**Goal:** Re-express B8g and B9 in coordinates production actually has, and report honestly
what survives the change.

**Why this is cycle 4 and not cycle 5.** It fixes a defect cycle 4 discovered in itself. Same
spec (`26.cycle4-target-selection.md`), same corpus, same definitions, same document
(`l2-targets.md`). A new cycle would split the target story across two specs for no gain.

**Complexity:** ⚠️ Medium — no new detectors, no new corpus. One anchor change, propagated.

---

## The defect

Every distance in cycle 4 is measured from the **move origin**, which the skeleton defines as
"the last counter-direction extreme before the move that the move never exceeded." That is
knowable only afterwards. `derive_facts._dol_menu` measures `abs(price - now_price)` from the
price at the call. Step 0 of the change protocol rejected B9 on exactly this.

The defect does not hit the two rules equally, and that asymmetry is the reason to expect a
result rather than a rerun:

- **B8g's feature is anchor-free.** Its gaps are pool-to-pool distances. They do not reference
  the origin, so they should port unchanged — and if they do not, something else is wrong and
  we want to know.
- **B9's feature is anchor-bound.** `d0` is origin-relative. Cycle 4 measured the size of the
  problem: MNQ's median distance to the draw falls **210.5 → 127.8 points** between the origin
  and +2 minutes. A 2.0× threshold at the origin is roughly 1.2× at the fill.

**And it is more than a coordinate change.** At a later instant the *stack itself* differs:
A4 measured that 13% of MNQ draws and 30% of MES draws are already swept by +2 minutes. The
pool set the rule walks is not the same set, so eligibility has to be rebuilt, not rescaled.

---

## What changes and what does not

| | cycle 4 | plan 30 |
|---|---|---|
| anchor | move origin (lookahead) | **a fixed clock instant** (production-computable) |
| reference price | origin price | **`now_price` at that instant**, as `_dol_menu` uses |
| eligibility | unswept at the origin | **unswept at the instant** |
| direction | the skeleton's, assumed correct | unchanged — spec §1.1 assumes direction is given |
| the label | the draw, from the phase-2 corpus | **unchanged** |
| the families | B8g (gap ahead), B9 (`d0`) | **unchanged — no new families** |

**Why a fixed clock instant is the right replacement.** It is the only anchor production can
compute without knowing how the move turned out. The Executor arms at 09:20 and the move
overwhelmingly starts at the open — 54 of 87 primaries begin in the 09:30 bin and 83 of 87
within fifteen minutes (spec §2.5) — so a clock instant shortly after the open is close to the
structural anchor on most sessions and computable on all of them.

Instants tested: **09:32, 09:35, 09:40**. Reported separately, never pooled.

---

## The discipline this plan must hold

**This is a re-expression of fixed rules, not a new search.** The families are frozen from
plan 29 §B.2. Nothing new is tried. That matters for what the scoring means: re-expressing a
fixed rule in new coordinates carries far less selection risk than searching for one, so LOSO
on the 63 discovery sessions remains defensible here where a fresh search would not.

**One new free parameter, and it is declared: B9's threshold.** It cannot stay at 2.0 — that
number was fitted at a different anchor. It is re-picked by the same rule cycle 4 used (the
interior optimum, jointly across both instruments), and the whole curve is reported so the
choice is visible rather than asserted.

**The holdout is spent and stays spent.** It may be *reported* once as a secondary check, with
the second-use inflation declared in the same sentence. It is not the gate, and no threshold
may be chosen from it.

## RESULT (2026-09-05) — B8g ports, B9's threshold converts, and the anchor costs coverage

Discovery only. Families frozen. 189 bundles rebuilt across three instants.

### B8g's feature is anchor-free, as predicted

| | origin (cycle 4) | 09:32 | 09:35 | 09:40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MNQ, on usable sessions | 62.5% (30/48) | 62.8% (27/43) | 63.6% (28/44) | **67.5%** (27/40) |
| MES, on usable sessions | 68.2% (30/44) | 62.5% (20/32) | 64.5% (20/31) | 63.6% (21/33) |
| MNQ Brier | 0.1746 | 0.1705 | 0.1723 | 0.1722 |
| MES Brier | 0.1770 | 0.1955 | 0.1896 | 0.1921 |

The gap feature survives the anchor change on both instruments — MNQ holds or improves, MES
gives back a few points. **`l2-targets.md` §2.2 stands.** The rule was not an artefact of the
lookahead origin, which was the failure this plan was written to be able to report.

### But the honest number is over ALL sessions, and it falls

A clock anchor cannot name a target on a session whose draw has already been passed. Those
sessions are not missing; they are a coverage cost the origin-anchored study never paid.

| degenerate (draw already passed) | 09:32 | 09:35 | 09:40 |
|---|---|---|---|
| MNQ | 5/48 (10%) | 4/48 (8%) | 8/48 (17%) |
| **MES** | **12/44 (27%)** | **13/44 (30%)** | **11/44 (25%)** |

Recomputed over every session rather than the usable ones:

| act-equivalent | origin | 09:32 | 09:35 | 09:40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MNQ | 62.5% | 56.3% | **58.3%** | 56.3% |
| MES | 68.2% | 45.5% | 45.5% | **47.7%** |

**MNQ loses ~5 points to the anchor; MES loses ~21.** MES's draws sit nearer in normalised
terms (its reach-m optimum was 2.0 against MNQ's 3.0), so they are taken sooner, and a fixed
clock instant misses a quarter to a third of them. This is the same asymmetry A4 measured from
the origin (13% vs 30% at +2 min) and it does not improve with a later instant.

### B9's threshold converts to roughly what was predicted

The plan predicted that 2.0 × avg_1h at the origin is "roughly 1.2× at the fill". Measured, at
09:35 (act accuracy on the covered set):

| `d0` ≤ | MNQ | MES |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 69% (9/13) | 50% (3/6) |
| 0.75 | 63% (12/19) | 58% (7/12) |
| **1.0** | **67% (16/24)** | **74% (14/19)** |
| 1.5 | 67% (24/36) | 73% (16/22) |
| 2.0 | 62% (25/40) | 68% (17/25) |
| no abstain | 58% | 45% |

**The threshold moves from 2.0 to about 1.0**, which is the predicted conversion within its own
resolution. B9's mechanism survives translation; only its number was anchor-bound, exactly as
step 0 diagnosed.

### What these numbers are NOT

**They are discovery-grade and there is no holdout left to check them against.** Two parameters
were re-fitted here — the threshold *and* the instant — over 3 instants × 6 thresholds × 2
instruments, which is 36 cells. Cycle 4 measured what discovery optimism costs on exactly this
corpus: **about 15 points.** A 67–74% discovery figure here should be expected to land in the
low-to-mid 50s out of sample, which is precisely where Stage C landed.

So this plan resolves a *portability* question and settles nothing about accuracy. That is what
it was scoped to do, and the distinction has to survive into the document.
