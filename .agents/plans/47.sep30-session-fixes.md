## D1 — a broker stop moved by the operator never reached the agent

**What happened.** `trade.py update-sl` moved the BROKER stop (30840, then 30852) and
`position.json`'s stop. The Executor's simulated stop stayed at 30710, so when the broker
stop filled at 10:09:20 the agent kept a phantom long for 11 min 17 s.

**Fix — the stop goes THROUGH the agent, on the existing operator control channel.**

| # | Deliverable | File |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `KIND_SET_STOP = "set_stop"` | `agent/trader/operator_control.py` |
| 2 | `trade.py update-sl` appends a `set_stop` record after the broker update, only when the agent owns the dispatcher and the session folder exists | `trade.py` |
| 3 | The graft applies `set_stop` on the first TICK at or after its `created_at` while a position is open (not only on a bar close: the broker stop is live from the instant the CLI returns). Other kinds stay bar-close work and keep their order | `agent/trader/graft.py` |
| 4 | `Executor.set_stop_override` moves the simulated stop and records `stop_moved` (`reason: operator`) | `agent/trader/executor.py` |
| 5 | `MirroringOrderPort.adopt_stop`: moves the model's stop, sends NOTHING (the broker already has it). Deliberately not named `move_stop`, so plan 35's action A stays refused on the live port | `agent/trader/order_port.py` |
| 6 | `OrderSim.move_stop`: `stop_moved` (hence `stop_out_initial`) only for a stop at or beyond the entry on the profit side; a moved stop on the losing side still books `stop_out` (an attempt, a cooldown) | `agent/trader/order_sim.py` |
| 7 | `stop_out_initial` in `CLOSE_REASONS` (without it the close is `untranslatable_event` and no order leaves) | `automation/agent_dispatch.py` |
| 8 | A profitable `stop_out_initial` latches `NO_ENTRY_AFTER_POSITIVE` | `agent/trader/executor.py` |
| 9 | Watchdog: record `stop_diverged` (once per pair, after a 10 s grace, record-only) when `position.json`'s stop and the modelled stop disagree | `automation/agent_dispatch.py` |
| 10 | session-analysis skill: pass `--operator-control` to the replay when the session has a control file | `.claude/skills/session-analysis/SKILL.md` |

Result: the Executor books the exit itself on the tick that touches the moved stop, sends
one market close (a no-op at the broker if the stop already filled, protective if it did
not), `position.json` clears, and the ledger carries the stop price.

**Known limit.** A touch in the ~1-2 s between the CLI's broker update and the tick that
applies the record, which then recovers, is still unseen.

## O3 — trailing stop study code (2026-10-01, flag OFF)

`agent/trader/trail.py` + `Executor._drive_trail`, behind `TRAIL_ENABLED = False`
(today's behaviour byte-identical). The operator's rule: arm at 50% of the way from the
entry to the bound T2; on that tick move the stop to break-even (`TRAIL_BE_AT_ARM`,
offset 0); on each NEW 1m continuation FVG (>= 5 pts) move the stop to 3 pts before the
gap one behind it; ratchet only. `scripts/ab_trail.py` runs it on the oracle-thesis rig
(arms A..H). Evidence so far: `<global>/sessions/2026-09-30/o3-trail-study.md` (Part 1
far-target oracle study; Part 2 real-T2 rig, trail -87.75 pts over 30 dates; Part 3
break-even at the mid, -38.5 pts over the 21 armed positions, no T2 winner lost). Not a
rule: not in `l2-mechanisms.md`, and the live port has no stop-modify path, so switching
the flag on live would only record `trail_unwired`.
