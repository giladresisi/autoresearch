## Executive Summary

The trader graft now carries a third number for an open position: the INITIAL target — the
structural level closest to the T2 target that still lies between the fill and it (offset 2 pts
toward the fill), or a synthetic 85 % fallback. A pure module selects it at the fill and flips a
one-way `reached` state on completed 1m bars (touch AND close beyond). The Executor records both
events, and behind `INITIAL_TARGET_ACTION` (default `"record"`) can move the stop to the initial
level once (A) or market-close on the first opposite 1m close (B); under `record` the tracker
keeps observing what A and B would have done, so a single record-only pass yields the A/B
counterfactuals. The 2026-09-18 replay reproduces the intended behaviour (selected at the 09:41
fill, reached on the 10:01 bar, nothing changes under `record`, footer A = B = booked +74.50).

## Implementation Summary

### §3.1 — Pure module `agent/trader/initial_target.py` (new)
- `select_initial_target(direction, entry, secondary, levels, attempts_used)`: candidates on the
  trade side of the fill, strictly between fill and T2, within `110 × 0.85^attempts` (floor 40);
  pick = candidate closest to T2; initial = pick ∓ 2 pts toward the fill if ≥ 40 pts from the
  fill; otherwise `synthetic_85pct` (`None` when 0.85·|T2 − P| < 40). Accepts `(name, price)`
  pairs or `{name, price}` dicts; junk rows skipped.
- `InitialTargetTracker.on_bar_close(bar) -> reached|None`: touch (`low ≤ initial` short /
  `high ≥` long) AND close beyond; flip is one-way.
- `InitialTargetTracker.post_flip(bar)`: after the flip, reports each counterfactual once and
  never on the flip bar — `cf_stop` (A: a touch of the initial level) and `cf_opp_close` (B: an
  opposite 1m close).
- Constants: `INITIAL_MAX_DIST_PTS=110`, `MIN_DIST_PTS=40`, `DIST_SHRINK_PCT=0.15`,
  `INITIAL_OFFSET_PTS=2`, `SYNTHETIC_RATIO=0.85`. No legacy imports (the
  `agent/trader/__init__.py` boundary); `test_gate_mechanisms.NEW_MODULES` extended so the
  structural gate covers it.

### §3.2 — Executor wiring (`agent/trader/executor.py`)
- `INITIAL_TARGET_ACTION = "record"` + `INITIAL_TARGET_ACTIONS` tuple, validated at import
  (a typo raises `ValueError` instead of silently running as `record`).
- Slots `_it` (live stage) and `_it_pending` (a stage whose exit bar is still to be judged when
  a re-fill lands in the same minute).
- `_arm_initial_target` called from `_set_target_on_fill`: builds the level universe via
  `target.level_universe`, selects, records `initial_target_selected {price, level, level_price,
  secondary, anchor, candidates, max_dist, action}`; resets `initial_target_error`.
- `_note_exit` at every exit site (`stop_out`, `take_profit`, `stop_out_initial`, `hard_close`,
  `user_close`) parks the stage as pending.
- `_drive_initial_target` + `_judge_initial_bar` run in `on_bar` step 2b — on completed 1m bars
  only, BEFORE the plan-death check, after OrderSim has already booked any same-bar stop
  (adverse-first, so the stop wins). Records `initial_target_reached {bar, price, position_open,
  action}` and the `initial_target_cf_stop` / `initial_target_cf_opp_close` counterfactuals.
- `_apply_initial_action` (A): `move_stop` exactly once, tighter-only, `stop_moved` latch.
- `_initial_opp_close` (B): market close on the first opposite close after the flip.

### §3.2 (support) — `agent/trader/target.py`
- `_bundle_for` one-slot memo keyed on frame identity (strong ref) + `now`, so the T2 pick and
  the initial pick at the same fill share one bundle build.
- `_validator_dict_for` memoised; `_running_block` exposes the in-progress 6h block's extremes.
- `level_universe(...)` = bundle levels + day/week high/low/mid + the running 6h block.
  Levels only — no FVG edges (see Divergence #3).

### §3.3 — Sim + mirror
- `order_sim.py`: `move_stop(now, price, *, level_name=None)`; a touch of a MOVED stop books
  `stop_out_initial`, not `stop_out`, so no attempt is spent and no cooldown/takeover window
  opens (a profit-side stop is not a failed entry).
- `broker_mirror.py`: `move_stop` → legacy `move-stop-exit` signal carrying
  `cautious_break_price`, `level="initial"`, `level_name`, which `live_orders.dispatch` maps to
  `update_stop_loss`; `initial_opp_close` → `market-close` with reason
  `initial-target-opp-close`; `stop_out_initial` → `stopped-out`. Nothing fires after a
  `user_close`.
- `records.py`: `initial_target_selected`, `initial_target_reached` builders.

- The flip is one-way.
- After the flip, `cf_stop` and `cf_opp_close` are each reported once and never on the flip bar.

  initial_target_reached) and `record` changes no order.
- A touch alone before the close-beyond bar does not flip.
- No stage is armed when the D1 menu yields no T2.
- Action A moves the stop exactly once and the moved stop then protects (touch →
  `stop_out_initial`, attempts_used 0, no cooldown/takeover window).
- A stage whose exit bar is still pending survives a same-minute re-fill (`_it_pending`).
- The `_bundle_for` memo matches on frame identity, not on a reused `id()`.
- Action B closes on the first opposite close after the flip and not before.
- A same-bar stop-out wins over the flip.
- The flip is still recorded on the bar that also reached T2 (`position_open false`).
- A bar completed before an exit on a later tick is still judged.
- A and B each reach the broker once and never after a `user_close` (end-to-end through the mirror).

Mirror / sim:
- `move_stop` emits the legacy `move-stop-exit` stop-update signal with `level="initial"`.
