## Executive Summary

A pure no-op refactor split the overloaded `FULFILL_PTS_MNQ`/`FULFILL_PTS_MES` constant family (read via `_fulfill_pts`) into three independent knobs — fulfillment (kept), depletion (`DEPLETE_PTS_*` / `_deplete_pts`), and departure (`DEPART_PTS_*` / `_depart_pts`) — so the depletion-retirement latch ("flood-stopper") can be tuned without moving the fulfillment threshold. The two new constant families are seeded byte-identical to `FULFILL_PTS_*`, making the split behavior-neutral by construction; 4 of the 6 `_fulfill_pts` call sites were migrated, 2 fulfillment sites retained. 14 new test instances pass, with zero new failures vs the Task-0 baseline.
